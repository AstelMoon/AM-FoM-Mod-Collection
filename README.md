### Better Animals
- More Heart Points
- More XP
- Faster Breeding
- Lowers Breeding Requirements
- Animals produce products more often
- Animals age up quicker
### Better Apiaries
- Better Honey Rewards
- Produces honey quicker
### Better Fruit Trees
- Produce more fruit
- Trees grow faster
- Regrow fruit faster
- Does not affect Pine and Oak trees
### Better Terrariums
- Better fish bait & bug pheromone rewards
- Produces items quicker
### Crops and Forageables
- Increases drop rate of crops, forageables and bushes
- Fruit Trees are not affected
- Options for Both, Only Crops and Only Bushes
### Wood and Stone
- Increases drop rate of rocks, branches, stumps, wood and hardwood
- Each tree growth stage drops more wood
- Affects all trees & stumps; deep woods and fruit trees included
- Does not affect rocks in the mines
- Options for Both, Only Wood and Only Rocks
